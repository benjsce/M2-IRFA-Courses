## Énoncé
Show that the following sets are convex:
1. the positive orthant $\mathbb{R}^d_{\geq0}=\{x\in\mathbb{R}^d:x_i\geq0\ \forall i\}$;
2. a hyperplane directed by $a\in\mathbb{R}^d$: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}^d:\langle a,x\rangle=b\}$, for $b\in\mathbb{R}$;
3. a half-space directed by $a\in\mathbb{R}^d$: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}^d:\langle a,x\rangle\leq b\}$, for $b\in\mathbb{R}$;
4. the unit ball of a norm $\|\cdot\|$ on $\mathbb{R}^d$: $\{x\in\mathbb{R}^d:\|x\|\leq1\}$.

## Solution officielle
None in the notes.

## Résolution
Take $x,y$ in the set and $\lambda\in[0,1]$, and write $z=\lambda x+(1-\lambda)y$. Everything rests on $\lambda\geq0$ and $1-\lambda\geq0$.

1. Each coordinate $z_i=\lambda x_i+(1-\lambda)y_i$ is a sum of two nonnegative terms, so $z_i\geq0$.
2. By linearity, $\langle a,z\rangle=\lambda\langle a,x\rangle+(1-\lambda)\langle a,y\rangle=\lambda b+(1-\lambda)b=b$.
3. The same computation with inequalities: $\langle a,z\rangle\leq\lambda b+(1-\lambda)b=b$, since the weights are nonnegative.
4. By the triangle inequality and homogeneity, $\|z\|\leq\lambda\|x\|+(1-\lambda)\|y\|\leq\lambda+(1-\lambda)=1$.

The orthant is also the intersection of the $d$ half-spaces $\{x_i\geq0\}$ (case 3 with $a=-e_i$, minus the $i$-th basis vector, and $b=0$), so it is convex by Proposition 2.1 without further work.

## Ce que l'exercice a révélé
- ods/convex-set: every case uses only that the two weights are nonnegative and sum to one — the defining property of a mix.
- ods/operations-on-convex-sets: once half-spaces are known to be convex, the orthant and the simplex follow by intersection.

## Contradiction avec la source
None. The letter $b$ is a scalar here, whereas it is a vector in least squares and in the quadratic form.
