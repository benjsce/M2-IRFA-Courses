## Énoncé
Number of iterations to reach a precision $10^{-6}$?

## Solution officielle
| $\kappa$ | CG | gradient descent |
|---|---|---|
| $10^2$ | $\approx70$ | $\approx700$ |
| $10^4$ | $\approx730$ | $\approx69\,000$ |
| $10^6$ | $\approx7\,300$ | $\approx6\,900\,000$ |

CG is not « a constant factor » better: the gap widens as the problem gets worse conditioned. These are worst-case bounds; a matrix whose eigenvalues are clustered is solved much faster than $\sqrt\kappa$ suggests.

## Résolution
Read « precision $10^{-6}$ » as dividing the error $\|x^k-x^\star\|_A$ by $10^6$.

For conjugate gradient, $2r^k\leq10^{-6}$ with $r=\tfrac{\sqrt\kappa-1}{\sqrt\kappa+1}$, so $k\geq\ln(2\times10^6)/\ln(1/r)$. Since $\ln(1/r)\approx2/\sqrt\kappa$ for large $\kappa$, $k\approx\tfrac{\sqrt\kappa}2\times14.5\approx7.25\sqrt\kappa$: $72$, $725$, $7\,254$.

For gradient descent, $\big(\tfrac{\kappa-1}{\kappa+1}\big)^k\leq10^{-6}$, $\ln\tfrac{\kappa+1}{\kappa-1}\approx2/\kappa$, so $k\approx\tfrac\kappa2\times13.8\approx6.9\,\kappa$: $691$, $69\,078$, $6\,907\,755$.

The slide rounds these to the values of the table.

## Ce que l'exercice a révélé
- ods/cg-convergence-rate: the factor 2 in front of the CG bound costs almost nothing ($\ln2$ added to $\ln10^6$); what matters is $\sqrt\kappa$ against $\kappa$.

## Contradiction avec la source
None: the exact bounds, computed here, agree with the rounded table.
