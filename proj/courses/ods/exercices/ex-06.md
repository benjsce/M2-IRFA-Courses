## Énoncé
Let $\hat{w},\hat{b}=\arg\min_{w,b}\tfrac12\|y-Xw-b\mathbf{1}_n\|^2+\tfrac\lambda2\|w\|^2$, $\lambda>0$, with $\bar{y}\in\mathbb{R}$ the mean of $y$ and $\overline{X}\in\mathbb{R}^p$ the mean of each column of $X$. Show that $\hat{b}=-\overline{X}^\top\hat{w}+\bar{y}$.

## Solution officielle
The second view of the slide gives two ways to deal with the intercept: for dense data, center $y$ and each column of $X$ and solve $\min_w\tfrac12\|y-Xw\|^2+\tfrac\lambda2\|w\|^2$; for sparse data, add a column of ones to $X$ and try not to penalize it, or not too much.

## Résolution
The objective is convex and differentiable in $(w,b)$, so at the minimizer its partial derivative in $b$ vanishes. Only the fit term depends on $b$: $\partial_b=-\mathbf{1}_n^\top(y-X\hat{w}-\hat{b}\mathbf{1}_n)=-(n\bar{y}-n\overline{X}^\top\hat{w}-n\hat{b})$. Setting it to zero gives $\hat{b}=\bar{y}-\overline{X}^\top\hat{w}$.

Substituting back: $y-Xw-\hat{b}\mathbf{1}_n=(y-\bar{y}\mathbf{1}_n)-(X-\mathbf{1}_n\overline{X}^\top)w$, the residual of the centered data. So $\hat{w}$ is the ridge solution on centered data, which is option 1.

On the four observations with $\lambda=1$: $\overline{X}=(\tfrac34,\tfrac12)$, $\bar{y}=\tfrac12$, the centered problem gives $\hat{w}=(-\tfrac2{13},\tfrac6{13})$ and $\hat{b}=\tfrac5{13}$. Notebook 2 computes option 1 with `linalg.solve` and option 2 with the penalty matrix `np.diag([alpha, 0.])`, which leaves the column of ones unpenalized; both give the same fit.

## Ce que l'exercice a révélé
- ods/intercept: the formula holds because $b$ is not penalized; with a penalty on $b$ the partial derivative gains a term $\lambda b$ and the fitted line no longer passes through the point of means.

## Contradiction avec la source
None. Notebook 2 writes the penalty with `alpha` where the slides write $\lambda$, and its option 1 writes the objective with $b$ while solving the centered problem without it.
