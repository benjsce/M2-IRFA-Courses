## Énoncé
Using $g^k=g^{k-1}-\beta_{k-1}Ad^{k-1}$, $\langle g^k,g^{k-1}\rangle=0$ and $\langle g^k,d^k\rangle=\|g^k\|^2$, show that $\alpha_k=-\dfrac{\langle g^k,Ad^{k-1}\rangle}{\langle d^{k-1},Ad^{k-1}\rangle}=\dfrac{\|g^k\|^2}{\|g^{k-1}\|^2}$.

## Solution officielle
The second view states the consequences: $A$ has disappeared, the direction coefficient is only a ratio of gradient norms; the same identity gives $\beta_k=\|g^k\|^2/\langle d^k,Ad^k\rangle$; only the step still involves $A$, and replacing it by a line search makes the recursion apply to any smooth $f$. It warns that Nocedal and Wright use $\alpha_k$ for the step and $\beta_k$ for the direction coefficient.

## Résolution
From the update, $Ad^{k-1}=(g^{k-1}-g^k)/\beta_{k-1}$, with $\beta_{k-1}\neq0$ while the algorithm runs.

Numerator: $\langle g^k,Ad^{k-1}\rangle=\big(\langle g^k,g^{k-1}\rangle-\|g^k\|^2\big)/\beta_{k-1}=-\|g^k\|^2/\beta_{k-1}$.

Denominator: $\langle d^{k-1},Ad^{k-1}\rangle=\big(\langle d^{k-1},g^{k-1}\rangle-\langle d^{k-1},g^k\rangle\big)/\beta_{k-1}=\|g^{k-1}\|^2/\beta_{k-1}$, using $\langle g^{k-1},d^{k-1}\rangle=\|g^{k-1}\|^2$ and $\langle g^k,d^{k-1}\rangle=0$ (slide 22).

The ratio, with its minus sign, is $\|g^k\|^2/\|g^{k-1}\|^2$. For the step, $\beta_k=\langle g^k,d^k\rangle/\langle Ad^k,d^k\rangle=\|g^k\|^2/\langle d^k,Ad^k\rangle$.

Check on the four observations: $\|g^1\|^2=\tfrac5{16}$, $\|g^0\|^2=5$, ratio $\tfrac1{16}$, the value that conjugate gradient computed with $A$.

## Ce que l'exercice a révélé
- ods/fletcher-reeves-formula: the denominator also needs $\langle g^k,d^{k-1}\rangle=0$, a fourth fact that the statement does not list.

## Contradiction avec la source
The statement lists three identities but the derivation also uses $\langle g^k,d^{k-1}\rangle=0$, proved on slide 22; it is not an error, only an omission in the hint.
