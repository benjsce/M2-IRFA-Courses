## Énoncé
Add support for the intercept (careful: non-regularized).

## Solution officielle
None; the notebook ends on this question.

## Résolution
As written, the notebook's objective $f(w)=\sum_i\log(1+e^{-y_ix_i^\top w})$ has no regularization, and a column of ones is already appended to $X$: the third weight is already an unpenalized intercept. The question makes sense for the penalized version, which is also the one that has a minimizer on these data.

Write the score $x_i^\top w+b$ with $x_i$ the two sepal measurements, and penalize $w$ only:
$$f(w,b)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\log\big(1+e^{-y_i(x_i^\top w+b)}\big)+\frac\lambda2\|w\|^2.$$
With $s_i=1/\big(1+e^{\,y_i(x_i^\top w+b)}\big)$, the gradient is $\nabla_wf=-\sum_iy_is_ix_i+\lambda w$ and $\partial_bf=-\sum_iy_is_i$, with no $\lambda b$ term. In code: keep the column of ones in `X`, and add `lam * w` to the gradient with the last entry set to zero, `lam * np.r_[w[:-1], 0.]`, and `lam / 2 * np.sum(w[:-1] ** 2)` to the objective.

Why the penalty matters here: the 100 flowers are linearly separable on the two sepal measurements (a linear program finds a separating $w$), so the unpenalized loss can be driven to $0$ by scaling $w$, and has no minimizer; `fmin_cg`, rerun here with SciPy 1.11, stops at $\|w\|\approx275$ with $f\approx4.5\times10^{-6}$. With $\lambda>0$ and $w$ penalized, $f$ becomes coercive in $w$; it is not strictly penalized in $b$, but $b$ alone cannot drive the loss to zero when both labels are present, so a minimizer exists.

## Ce que l'exercice a révélé
- ods/logistic-regression: on separable data, the unpenalized problem has no minimizer, and the solver's answer depends only on its stopping tolerance.
- ods/intercept: the rule « do not penalize the intercept » carries over from ridge to logistic regression unchanged.

## Contradiction avec la source
The question asks for a non-regularized intercept, but the objective of the notebook carries no regularization at all, and already includes a column of ones: as posed, there is nothing to add. The exercise is read here as asking for the penalized version.
