## Énoncé
For $\min_{w\in\mathbb{R}^p}\tfrac12\|y-Xw\|^2+\tfrac\lambda2\|w\|^2$:
- show that ridge regression boils down to the minimization of a quadratic form;
- propose a closed-form solution;
- show that the solution is obtained by solving a linear system;
- is the objective function strongly convex?
- assuming $n<p$, what is the value of the constant of strong convexity?

## Résolution
**Quadratic form.** Expanding, $\tfrac12\|y-Xw\|^2+\tfrac\lambda2\|w\|^2=\tfrac12w^\top(X^\top X+\lambda I_p)w-(X^\top y)^\top w+\tfrac12\|y\|^2$: a quadratic form with matrix $X^\top X+\lambda I_p$, linear term $X^\top y$ and constant $\tfrac12\|y\|^2$.

**Linear system and closed form.** Its gradient is $(X^\top X+\lambda I_p)w-X^\top y$; it vanishes on the linear system $(X^\top X+\lambda I_p)w=X^\top y$, whose matrix is invertible for $\lambda>0$, so $\hat{w}=(X^\top X+\lambda I_p)^{-1}X^\top y$.

**Strong convexity.** The Hessian $X^\top X+\lambda I_p$ has eigenvalues $\sigma_i^2+\lambda\geq\lambda>0$, so the objective is strongly convex with constant $\lambda_{\min}(X^\top X)+\lambda=\sigma_{\min}^2+\lambda$.

**Case $n<p$.** $X^\top X$ is $p\times p$ of rank at most $n<p$, so it has a zero eigenvalue: the constant is exactly $\lambda$.

On the four observations ($n=4>p=2$), $\lambda=1$: the matrix is $\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\1&3\end{pmatrix}$, the constant $\tfrac{7-\sqrt5}2\approx2.38$, and $\hat{w}=(\tfrac1{11},\tfrac7{11})$.

## Ce que l'exercice a révélé
- ods/ridge-regression: when $n<p$, the penalty alone provides the strong convexity; without it the minimizer would not be unique.
